Example 1: Conducting thorough research on visual aspects of the branding project.

Action

To ensure that the visual style of the branding project aligned with Woody Veneman's vision, I embarked on an extensive research process. My first step was to explore Woody’s website, analyzing his old music covers, album art, and posters. However, none of these really captured the unique vision I wanted to reflect in the new project. I also browsed his art gallery page, but again, the materials didn’t provide the strong inspiration I was looking for.
Next, I researched the work of Truus de Groot, his band partner in the Veneman en de Groot project. Her album covers and visuals were more abstract, powerfully colored, and visually striking, offering a direction that seemed more fitting. Her work, with its intense hues and energetic presentation, sparked ideas for a new aesthetic that better matched the duo’s vision.

The real breakthrough came when Woody introduced me to one of his favorite artists on Instagram, @ramusai. His work featured edgy, obscure imagery, with bold colors and elements of Goth and cybersigilism. The dramatic, eye-catching style was a perfect match for the emotional intensity of the music. This influence allowed me to incorporate similar stylistic choices into the project, bringing out the raw, energetic, and sometimes abstract qualities that aligned with both Woody’s music and his artistic preferences.

Another key part of my research process involved the lyrics. By listening to the songs repeatedly, I tried to feel the music and imagine visuals that would resonate with the themes, tones, and emotions present in the tracks. This deep dive into both the music and visual influences helped me create posters and covers that reflected the soul of the project.

Result

The mid-project presentation marked a significant moment where the new aesthetic was revealed to Woody and the team. We moved away from the darker dive-bar-themed visuals, opting for a more vibrant color palette consisting of purples, pinks, and blues. This new abstract style was much more in line with the duo's modern "neo-smartlappen" genre and was well-received by the client. The feedback from Woody was overwhelmingly positive, and he particularly appreciated the way the visuals connected with the emotional tone of the music.

Feedback

The shift to a more abstract and colorful imagery style was not just accepted but praised. Woody highlighted how the new design truly captured the vibe he wanted to project for the duo’s modern take on Dutch smartlappen. The feedback during the mid-project review confirmed that the artistic direction was a success, as it better aligned with his vision than the original dark, bar-themed concept. The client appreciated my research and willingness to explore various artistic avenues to reach a visual style that connected with the essence of the music. The exploration of Ramusai's art, in particular, played a crucial role in refining the final design, and Woody noted that it felt much more in tune with the energy of the music.

Reflection

This experience taught me a critical lesson about how important it is too deeply connected with the client’s artistic vision and to remain flexible throughout the creative process. I learned that research isn’t just about gathering visual references—it’s about truly understanding the emotion, story, and personality behind the project. This deeper connection allowed me to pivot from an initial concept that was safe and familiar to a more daring and unique design that resonated with Woody's style. Additionally, I learned that it’s crucial not to get too attached to early ideas or concepts, as feedback or new inspiration may take the project in a completely different direction. This adaptability and willingness to explore new creative paths ultimately led to a stronger final result.

Decide

Moving forward, I plan to incorporate even more of this adaptive and research-driven approach into my future projects. I’ll make sure to stay open to feedback, even when it challenges my initial ideas, and I’ll dig deeper into understanding the essence of the project. This experience has shown me the value of thorough research and how important it is to not only understand the client’s vision but also to push myself creatively to meet and exceed those expectations. I’ll continue to apply this mindset of flexibility, research, and client alignment in all my branding and visual projects.

Example 2: Improving my planning skills, ensuring that I could manage tasks efficiently and achieve steady progress.

Action

To achieve my learning goal, I used Trello boards to organize tasks and deadlines. I created separate boards for each project phase, which helped me track my progress effectively. The Trello boards allowed me to categorize tasks into lists such as "To Do," "In Progress," "Feedback," and "Finished," enabling a clear overview of what needed to be done and what had already been completed.
Additionally, I scheduled regular checkpoints and set specific deadlines for important tasks, like completing the design iterations, gathering feedback, and refining both web and mobile designs. By organizing tasks into manageable steps, I could work systematically and avoid last-minute pressure.

Result

Following this structured planning approach allowed me to stay on track and make consistent progress. By adhering to my schedule, I was able to complete key project milestones, including design phases and content strategies, without falling behind. The organized workflow also helped me remain focused on the priorities, ensuring each project element received adequate attention. As a result, I was able to reach an advanced level of completion on my tasks, enabling me to meet project requirements effectively.
My semester coach provided positive feedback, noting that I was doing well with my grades and acknowledging my efforts to enhance user interactivity in my portfolio. This feedback validated that my planning approach was helping me stay on the right track, and it motivated me to continue refining my workflow.

Feedback

The feedback from my semester coach highlighted that I was progressing well and suggested exploring more options for interacting with my portfolio. This motivated me to create a drop-down menu for learning outcomes, adding another level of interactivity and enhancing the overall user experience. I also took note of the suggestion to start researching options for future semesters, which I planned to incorporate into my longer-term planning.

Reflection

Looking back, I realize that a well-thought-out plan is crucial for staying organized and focused, especially in complex projects. The Trello boards made it easier to visualize the workflow and allowed me to adjust tasks as necessary. This experience showed me the value of breaking down tasks into manageable steps and sticking to a timeline, which not only helps in avoiding last-minute work but also provides a structured way to integrate feedback.
For future projects, I will continue using Trello or similar tools to plan my workflow. I also aim to incorporate regular reflection points within my planning, allowing me to reassess my progress periodically and make necessary adjustments. Additionally, I plan to explore more advanced project management techniques, such as timeboxing, to further improve my productivity.

Decide

Moving forward, I will prioritize structured planning in all my projects. I’ve seen firsthand how a well-organized workflow can improve efficiency and overall output quality. My next step is to apply this planning method to other areas, focusing on balancing creativity with structured work processes. Additionally, I’ll keep refining my interactivity ideas for the portfolio to align with my coach's feedback, ensuring it meets both user expectations and project goals.

Example 4: Stakeholder interaction during the first presentation (Branding Project)

Action

At the start of the branding project, I took the initiative to create the stylescape and moodboard for our client, Woody Veneman. Drawing from the initial information I had, I incorporated elements inspired by old dive bars, neon lights, and vivid colors. Before presenting, I sought feedback from a teacher to refine my ideas further. During our first presentation on September 18th, I showcased my work to Woody, but it became clear that my interpretation did not fully align with his vision. Woody emphasized that while he drew inspiration from dive bars, his core themes revolved around love and a neo-vibe, reflecting the essence of his music. This feedback shifted my perspective, and within two days, I revised the moodboard, incorporating his preferences. The updated version was presented in the following meeting and was met with great enthusiasm from Woody.

Result

The final moodboard resonated strongly with Woody’s vision, receiving positive feedback and approval to proceed in this direction. This not only set the tone for the branding project but also gave me and my team clear guidance for further design work. The client’s enthusiastic response reinforced the value of iterative design and stakeholder interaction.

Presentation Link

Feedback

The initial feedback from Woody highlighted the need for a deeper connection to the emotional and thematic core of his music, specifically the themes of love and neo-vibes. His approval of the updated moodboard affirmed the importance of understanding a client’s personal connection to their vision. Additionally, the visual impact of the updated moodboard set a strong foundation for the project's direction and inspired further work within the group.

Reflection

This experience taught me that aligning closely with a client's emotional and thematic goals is crucial for success. While I initially focused on the aesthetic aspects of dive bars and neon colors, the feedback reminded me to explore the deeper narrative behind the client’s vision. It also emphasized the importance of adaptability and effective time management, as I had only two days to deliver a significant revision. Furthermore, presenting directly to a stakeholder provided invaluable insights, highlighting the importance of clear communication and iterative design in client-based projects.

Decide

In future projects, I will prioritize direct communication with stakeholders early in the design process to gather more nuanced insights into their vision. I will also aim to validate initial concepts more thoroughly by sharing drafts and gathering feedback incrementally. Additionally, I plan to allocate more buffer time for revisions, ensuring I can adapt quickly to unexpected feedback without compromising quality.
