Learning outcome 5 Personal Leadership

Example 1: Pivoting my portfolio design and development in a new direction.

Action

Starting from the second week of the semester, I began designing my portfolio. After researching other portfolios, I decided to create an interface similar to YouTube’s, where my learning outcomes and projects would be displayed as "videos" with the documentation shown in the comment section. I received positive feedback on the initial concept and began coding it using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I laid out the base structure, added a few learning outcomes, and implemented small JavaScript animations.

To get further feedback, I presented the portfolio to Stan, the teacher who oversees most of our JavaScript classes. While the code and design were solid, he pointed out that the YouTube interface might be too common and doesn’t align with the objectives of a personal portfolio. He suggested I aim for a more impactful design, given my capabilities. Reflecting on that feedback, I spent the next weekend redesigning my portfolio. I pivoted to a 3D space theme using Three.js, featuring static and moving stars, a planet, and two constellations that represent learning outcomes and projects. The user can navigate through the space with keyboard and mouse controls, and there’s a menu for switching between the main planet and the constellations.

Result

The new portfolio, with its immersive space theme and interactive elements, was well-received when I showcased it. The feedback was largely positive, with appreciation for the unique, creative approach. The 3D navigation system and visual representation of learning outcomes and projects made the portfolio stand out. However, it was noted that I still needed to find a more effective way to display the learning outcomes examples.

Feedback

The feedback I received was encouraging. The teacher appreciated the originality of the new design and the technical complexity of the space interface. The interactive features and navigation system were seen as engaging and well-implemented. The main suggestion was to think more about how I could better present the actual content, particularly the learning outcomes, within this new format.

Reflection

This experience highlighted how crucial it is to align design choices with the portfolio's purpose and to push beyond common solutions. Initially, I gravitated toward a familiar interface, but the feedback helped me see the value in taking a more unique approach. Reflecting on the shift, I realized the importance of balancing creativity with functionality and ensuring the design effectively showcases the content, not just the visuals.

Decide

In the future, I will aim to create portfolio designs that are both innovative and highly functional. I learned that it’s important to be open to feedback, especially when it challenges my initial ideas, as it can lead to more impactful results. I will continue iterating on the content presentation within my current portfolio, focusing on how to clearly and effectively display learning outcomes and project details in an engaging way.

Example 2: Applying new software tools such as Photoshop, After Effects, and Blender.

Action

During the initial project, Veneman en de Groot, I began using Photoshop for small tasks like color adjustments and cropping images. This helped me familiarize myself with the interface and basic functionality, but I wanted to explore the software’s full potential. I started experimenting on my own, aiming to create original visuals that went beyond simple edits.

As I progressed, I became interested in creating animated visuals, which led me to Adobe After Effects. My first attempt was a moody scene of a rainy street, complete with a couple of building blocks and neon lights flickering on and off. This was an exciting challenge, as it required understanding layers, keyframes, and effects in After Effects to bring my vision to life.

Later, as my portfolio reached a solid foundation, I received feedback suggesting I focus more on visual elements to make it stand out. This feedback motivated me to try Blender, where I started working on creating realistic planet surfaces and textures. Learning Blender was a significant step forward, as it introduced me to 3D modeling and texturing, which are valuable skills for enhancing the visual depth of my work.
Through this journey, I discovered the importance of self-motivation and perseverance in learning complex software. I also received peer feedback on my work, and the response was overwhelmingly positive, validating my efforts and encouraging me to keep improving.

Result

The results of my work were visually appealing and contributed significantly to my portfolio. The moody, rainy street scene in After Effects showcased my ability to create atmospheric animations, while my Blender experiments with planet surfaces demonstrated my progress in 3D modeling. This exploration of new software tools not only improved the quality of my work but also expanded my skill set, making me more versatile and capable of tackling diverse visual projects in the future.
The positive peer feedback I received confirmed that my visual skills were developing well and that I was on the right track. This recognition motivated me to continue experimenting with these tools and take on more challenging visuals in upcoming projects.

Feedback

I received feedback suggesting that I focus on the visual aspects of my portfolio after establishing a strong foundational structure. This advice encouraged me to explore tools like Blender to enhance the visual impact of my work. My peers also provided feedback on the visuals I created, noting the quality and creativity of the scenes. This positive feedback reinforced my confidence and inspired me to keep improving my visual skills.

Reflection

Reflecting on this experience, I learned that mastering new software independently requires dedication and a willingness to experiment. Overcoming challenges with each tool helped me build a diverse skill set:
○ Photoshop: Image editing fundamentals.
○ After Effects: Dynamic animations.
○ Blender: Basic 3D modeling.
○ Self-led exploration has been essential for my personal growth, allowing me to work at my own pace and explore areas of interest freely.

Future Goals:

○ Continue developing skills in After Effects (complex animations) and Blender (advanced modeling).
○ Integrate these skills into collaborative projects, learning from others and applying knowledge in new contexts.

Decide

Going forward, I will continue enhancing my visual skills by dedicating time to learn advanced techniques in Photoshop, After Effects, and Blender. I plan to set specific goals for each software, such as creating a full 3D scene in Blender or experimenting with lighting effects in After Effects. Additionally, I aim to seek feedback regularly from peers and mentors to ensure my skills are evolving in the right direction. This journey has shown me the value of taking initiative in personal development, and I am excited to keep pushing my creative boundaries.

Example 3: Following Feedback and implement as much as possible in my work

Action

My goal was to incorporate feedback into my work, so I began by reviewing and reflecting on the feedback from my last portfolio review. After careful analysis, I identified the necessary changes:
○ Simplify navigation to improve clarity and usability for new users.
○ Document user testing outcomes, highlighting pain points and areas for improvement.
○ Reduce the number of clicks to access key sections like learning outcomes.
○ Use GitLab to manage and showcase code changes, linking repositories to my portfolio.
○ Provide descriptive commit messages and snapshots to illustrate the development process, including summaries of stakeholder presentations and feedback.
I tackled these issues one by one, starting with resolving the navigation, which I completed quickly. I then began documenting the user tests for both the mobile version prototype of the CZ website and the "fill in your details" page for our development project.

Moving on I modified my Learning Outcome 2 documentation on version control and git to showcase better what I did and also to link my git in my portfolio.
