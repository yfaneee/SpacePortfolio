Veneman en de Groot - Branding Project

Introduction

Woody Veneman and Truus de Groot, both deeply rooted in Dutch music and culture, are on a mission to capture the soul of a vanishing scene. As dive bars across the country close their doors, Woody and Truus feel the urgency to preserve the raw, heartfelt atmosphere that only these spaces can offer. Their vision centers around the dive bar's emotional core, but they face a challenge: creating a brand that reflects this world with authenticity. They need a powerful, memorable identity that not only celebrates these beloved spaces but also keeps their spirit alive in a rapidly changing landscape.

Moodboard

The moodboad, which featured a more intimate and emotional color scheme, was well received by Woody during the mid-project presentation. He appreciated the thematic toward a softer, love-centered design, which resonated more with his vision for the project.

Stylescape

Woody provided feedback after the mid-project presentation, expressing his satisfaction with the shift in direction. He pointed out that the new colors and mood aligned better with his vision for neo-smartlappen. He encouraged further exploration of the "love" theme, which led to further refinement of the stylescape to ensure it matched his evolving vision.

Content strategy

The goal is to create an engaging pub crawl event that promotes Woody & Truus' neo-smartlappen music style, builds community, and connects with a 20-25-year-old audience both in person and online. The strategy includes raising awareness through a mix of digital and physical promotion and fostering meaningful interactions with fans.
Audience: Primarily 20-25-year-old music lovers interested in niche genres like Smartlappen and alternative social scenes like dive bars. This group is active in pubs and on social platforms, especially Instagram and TikTok.
Content:
Video: Live shows, music videos, and vlogs.
Images: Vintage-style photos in dive bars.
Text: Blog posts, song lyrics, artist interviews.
Audio: Acoustic song versions and genre-focused podcasts.
Physical: Posters, merch, and stickers.
Distribution Plan:
Social Media: Use Instagram and TikTok for promotional videos, song snippets, and hashtag campaigns (#zonderRafelsGeenZiel, #smartlappen).
Collaborations: Engage influencers and friends to boost reach.
Physical: Place posters and stickers at event venues (especially Stratumseind), hand out stickers as pre-event promotions, and offer giveaways to encourage fan sharing.

Reflection

This project taught me the value of deeply understanding and aligning with a client’s vision. Immersing myself in Woody & Truus' music allowed me to capture the emotional depth they wanted in the stylescape. This experience underscored the importance of staying open to feedback, which ultimately enriched the design.
I learned that flexibility and iteration are key to delivering a product that genuinely resonates with the client. Moving forward, I plan to invest more time in exploring the client’s goals from the outset. This will help me build a stronger foundation early in the process and ensure alignment.
Additionally, this project sparked a desire to expand my skills by learning software like Illustrator, Photoshop, and After Effects. Mastering these tools will empower me to create even more dynamic and impactful designs in the future.
